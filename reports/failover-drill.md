The rows the old primary accepted after the promotion are gone for good: pg_rewind replaces its diverged pages with the new primary's. In a real failover the same happens to every write that reaches the old primary once the standby is promoted, which is why the old primary must be fenced (stopped, or cut off from clients) before the promotion. The planned switchover drill stops it first and loses nothing.
